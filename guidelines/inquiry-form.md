# TRD Creative Studio — Project Inquiry Form Brief

## Objective

The inquiry form is used to qualify potential clients before moving into a discovery call.

The goal is to:

- Understand what the client needs
- Identify the relevant TRD service
- Understand project scope and urgency
- Check budget fit
- Reduce unnecessary discovery calls
- Make the next step clear for qualified leads

Recommended flow:

Website → Inquiry Form → Qualification → Discovery Call → Proposal

---

# Discovery Call Logic

The discovery call should not be the default first step for every website visitor.

Recommended flow:

### Cold / Website Lead

Inquiry Form  
→ Internal Review  
→ Qualified  
→ Discovery Call  
→ Proposal

### Warm Lead / Referral

Direct Discovery Call can still be shared manually.

Examples:

- Existing client referral
- Previous conversation
- LinkedIn / Threads discussion
- Returning client
- Partner introduction

# UX Principles

## Keep the Form Short

Target completion time:

**2–3 minutes**

Avoid asking for:

- Full project specifications
- Detailed feature lists
- Complete sitemap
- Full brand guidelines
- Technical architecture
- Detailed research documents

Those can be discussed after qualification.

---

## Use Progressive Disclosure

Only show relevant questions after the user selects a service.

Example:

Website selected  
→ show website type

Product selected  
→ show product engagement

AI Video selected  
→ show video type

This keeps the form shorter and more relevant.

---

## Make Budget Required

Budget helps TRD qualify leads before using time for a discovery call.

Use ranges rather than an open text field.

---

## Avoid Over-qualifying

The inquiry form should answer:

1. Who are they?
2. What do they need?
3. What problem are they trying to solve?
4. What is the expected budget?
5. When do they want to start?

The discovery call handles everything deeper.

# Internal Qualification Criteria

Before inviting a lead to a discovery call, review:

- Service fit
- Problem clarity
- Budget fit
- Timeline feasibility
- Strategic value
- Current studio capacity
- Client responsiveness / seriousness

Suggested internal outcome:

- Qualified
- Need More Information
- Not a Fit
- Follow Up Later

---

# Positioning Principle

The form should communicate:

> TRD works selectively and wants to understand the project before starting a conversation.

It should not feel like:

> Fill in a long procurement form before you can talk to us.

Keep it professional, short, and easy to complete.
